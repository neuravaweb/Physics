Section 7 · Problem 2

Area from two independent measurements.

PROPAGATION OF ERROR II
L = (15.3 ± 0.1) cm · W = (8.4 ± 0.1) cm
A = L × W

When two independent quantities are multiplied, their relative uncertainties add in quadrature, not directly. The wide side contributes less in fractional terms than the narrow side — this is why the width’s error dominates here.

Step 01 — Measurements

Two rulers, two uncertainties.

L = 15.3 cm, ΔL = ± 0.1 cm
W = 8.4 cm, ΔW = ± 0.1 cm
δL/L = 0.00654 · δW/W = 0.01190
Step 02 — Central value

Multiply for the area.

A = L × W = 15.3 × 8.4
A = 128.52 cm²
Step 03 — Propagate in quadrature

For products, fractions add as squares.

(ΔA/A)² = (ΔL/L)² + (ΔW/W)²
= (0.00654)² + (0.01190)²
= 4.27e-5 + 1.42e-4 = 1.84e-4
ΔA/A = 0.01358 → 1.36 %
ΔA = 0.01358 × 128.52 = 1.745 cm²
Reported value
A = 128.5 ± 1.7 cm²

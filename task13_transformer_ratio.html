PROBLEM

How many secondary turns?

A transformer steps 120 V AC down to 9 V AC. The primary coil has 400 turns. Find N₂.

RELATIONSHIP

Voltages and turns scale together:

V₁ / V₂ = N₁ / N₂

Solve for N₂:

N₂ = N₁ · (V₂ / V₁)
SUBSTITUTE
N₂ = 400 · (9 / 120) = 400 · 0.075 = 30 turns

The watch the secondary coil grow loop-by-loop on the right leg of the core.

SECONDARY TURN COUNT
30turns
VERIFY THE RATIO
N₁ : N₂ = 400 : 30 = 40 : 3 V₁ : V₂ = 120 : 9 = 40 : 3 ✓
WHY IT WORKS

Each turn of either coil sees the same changing magnetic flux through the core. So the EMF per turn is identical on both sides — the only thing that differs is how many turns you sum across.

1 turn → 0.3 V (here). 400 of those gives 120 V. 30 of those gives 9 V. Same volts-per-turn, different counts.

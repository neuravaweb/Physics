PROBLEM

Power & Heat

A resistor of R = 100 Ω has V = 50 V applied across it. Find the dissipated power, then the energy converted to heat in 5 minutes.

POWER

Three equivalent forms of Joule's law:

P = V·I (general) P = I²·R (known I) P = V²/R (known V — our case)
P = (50)² / 100 = 2500 / 100 = 25 W
ENERGY OVER 5 MIN

For constant power, energy is just power × time:

E = P · t t = 5 min = 300 s P = 25 W E = 25 · 300 = 7 500 J = 7.5 kJ
ENERGY DISSIPATED · 5 MIN
7.5kJ
PHYSICAL PICTURE

Electrons crash into lattice ions and lose kinetic energy as heat. The resistor warms up — the visual color shift on the left tracks that heating.

7.5 kJ is roughly the energy needed to heat 20 g of water from 0 °C to boiling, or to lift a 1 kg book about 760 m. Not lightning — but it's continuous, and it accumulates.

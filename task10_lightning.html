PROBLEM

Lightning Bolt

A lightning bolt transfers 30 C of charge from cloud to ground in 2 ms. What is the average current of the bolt?

DEFINITION

Average current is total charge passing per unit time:

I_avg = ΔQ / Δt

This is the time-averaged current — actual lightning has a sharp peak well above the average.

PLUG IN NUMBERS
ΔQ = 30 C Δt = 2 ms = 2 × 10⁻³ s I_avg = 30 C / 0.002 s = 15 000 A = 15 kA
AVERAGE CURRENT
15kA

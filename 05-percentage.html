Section 7 · Problem 5

What a four-percent stopwatch looks like.

PERCENTAGE UNCERTAINTY
t = 5.45 ± 0.22 s
δt/t = ?

Convert an absolute uncertainty back into a percentage of the measured value. This is the standard way to compare two measurements made with different instruments — you compare the fractions, not the raw deltas.

Step 01 — Read absolute uncertainty

The instrument tells you ± 0.22 s.

t = 5.45 s
Δt = ± 0.22 s
Step 02 — Divide

Percentage = (absolute / value) × 100.

δt/t = 0.22 / 5.45
δt/t = 0.04037
Step 03 — Express as percent

Multiply by 100.

δt/t × 100 = 4.04 %
Percentage uncertainty
≈ 4 %

Section 7 · Problem 7

Eleven scores. Two statistics.

MEAN & STD DEVIATION
N = 11 → 9 (after trim)
sample std (N−1)

Mean is the centre of mass. Standard deviation is the typical distance from that centre. Trimming the highest and lowest values (a crude form of outlier rejection) makes the data tighter — the spread shrinks even though the mean barely moves.

Full Sample · N = 11Untrimmed.

scores = [88, 92, 79, 85, 95, 81, 86, 90, 83, 77, 89]
Σ x = 945
x̄ = 945 / 11 = 85.91
Σ (x − x̄)² = 310.91
σ = √(310.91 / 10) = 5.58
Result
x̄ = 85.91 · σ = 5.58

Trimmed Sample · N = 9Highest (95) & lowest (77) removed.

scores = [88, 92, 79, 85, 81, 86, 90, 83, 89]
Σ x = 945 − 95 − 77 = 773
x̄' = 773 / 9 = 85.89
Σ (x − x̄')² = 148.89
σ' = √(148.89 / 8) = 4.32
Result
x̄' = 85.89 · σ' = 4.32

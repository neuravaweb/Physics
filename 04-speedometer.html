Section 7 · Problem 4

The honest range of a 60 km/h reading.

RELATIVE UNCERTAINTY
ε = 5 %
v_read = 60 km/h

Relative uncertainty is the easiest kind: the meter says this number, and the manufacturer guarantees the truth is within a percentage of it. Multiply, get the bandwidth, draw the range.

Step 01 — Specification

Read the dial. Read the spec sheet.

v_read = 60 km/h
Δv / v = 5 %
Step 02 — Absolute uncertainty

Convert percent into km/h.

Δv = 0.05 × 60 = 3 km/h
Step 03 — Range

Subtract and add.

v_min = 60 − 3 = 57 km/h
v_max = 60 + 3 = 63 km/h
True speed is somewhere in
[57, 63] km/h
